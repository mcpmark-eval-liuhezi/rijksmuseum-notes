# Rembrandt Timeline

**Source:** Rijksmuseum collection records via the Rijksmuseum Data Services. All facts in this document are drawn directly from the museum's own collection records.

## Centerpiece — The Night Watch (Object SK-C-5)

- **Official title as catalogued by the Rijksmuseum:** *Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'*
- **Date on the record:** 1642 (record date 1642-01-01)
- **Collection record ID:** https://id.rijksmuseum.nl/200107928

## How many works the collection returns for Rembrandt

A collection search for **Rembrandt van Rijn** as involved maker returns **1,443 works**.
